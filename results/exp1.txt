EXPERIMENT 1 - Corpus Analysis.

Objectives:
The objective of this experiment is to Analyze the basic structural properties of a small text corpus, including the number of documents, sentences, tokens, and types, as well as word frequency and frequency distribution, to establish a foundational understanding of the dataset before any further processing.


Test Cases:
The corpus was tested on:
Simple declarative sentences
Sentence initial capitalization variants
Lexical repetition within a sentence
Enumeration/ comma-seperated lists
Compound/ technical noun phrases
Sentence-final punctuation

Observed Problems:
Naive whitespace tokenizer keeps punctuation attached to words, and counts them as separate types from their unpunctuated forms.
Case-sensitive splitting treats "A" and "a" as distinct types.
Both issues inflate the type count.

Research observation:
The high TTR (0.86) reflects the corpus's small size rather than true lexical richness- with only 49 tokens, most words occur once. Repeated tokens suggest the text Centers on NLP-related terminology.
